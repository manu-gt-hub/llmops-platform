# Acme Cloud — Service level agreement

## Uptime targets

- Starter: no SLA.
- Pro: 99.9% monthly uptime.
- Enterprise: 99.95% monthly uptime.

## Service credits

When the monthly uptime of a Pro or Enterprise workspace falls below the
target of its plan, the customer receives a credit on the next invoice:

| Monthly uptime                     | Credit |
|------------------------------------|--------|
| Below the plan target, 99.0% or more | 10%  |
| Below 99.0%, 95.0% or more         | 25%    |
| Below 95.0%                        | 50%    |

Credits must be requested within 30 days of the end of the affected month.
